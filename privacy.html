Legal & Transparency

Privacy Policy.

Last updated: August 2026. How ZYANIX respects and protects your information.

1. Information We Collect

When you contact ZYANIX through our website forms or direct email, we collect the information you voluntarily provide, including your full name, email address, company name, project domain, budget expectations, and project description.

We do not collect unnecessary personal tracking data, sell user information, or share details with third-party advertisers.

2. How We Use Your Information

The information you provide is used strictly to:

  • Evaluate technical feasibility and prepare project estimates
  • Draft your Signed MVP Scope specification document
  • Communicate with you regarding your inquiry or active engineering project
  • Deliver project milestones, invoices, and source code handovers

3. Client Code & Data Confidentiality

ZYANIX treats all client codebases, architectures, credentials, and business concepts with the strictest confidentiality. We routinely sign mutual Non-Disclosure Agreements (NDAs) prior to project commencement.

Client credentials and API secrets are never stored in public repositories or exposed to unauthorized parties.

4. Third-Party Services

Our website utilizes Cloudflare for edge security, DNS, and DDoS mitigation, as well as Formspree for contact form email routing. These service providers operate under strict data protection and encryption standards.

5. Contact Us

If you have any questions regarding this Privacy Policy or your personal data, please contact Founder & CEO Shubham Pathak directly via our contact form.
