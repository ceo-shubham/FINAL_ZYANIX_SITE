For eligible scoped projects (including conversion landing pages, MVP modules, and discrete web features defined in our Signed MVP Scope), we commit to a 7-consecutive-day turnaround. If we fail to deliver within this timeframe, the work is delivered completely free of charge. Full terms are specified in our agreement before commencement.

To eliminate financial risk for our clients, project fees are divided across three verifiable stages:

• 30% Kickoff: Upon signing the MVP Scope to begin architecture, design, and repository scaffolding.

• 30% Progress: When the core functional milestone is demonstrated live on our private staging server.

• 40% Delivery: Upon full QA testing, live production cutover, and complete GitHub repository transfer.

You retain 100% intellectual property ownership. Upon completion of the final milestone payment, all GitHub source code repositories, databases, configuration files, and deployment scripts are transferred directly to your organization. No royalties, no recurring lock-in.
