The Founder's Journey

Built by a builder,
not a middleman.

Before founding ZYANIX, Shubham established ZENSTRO IT Solutions, where he gained practical hands-on experience building, shipping, and maintaining web and software solutions across varied business domains.

Building on that solid operational foundation, he launched ZYANIX with a sharper, more ambitious vision: to build one of India's most trusted, full-service technology companies — delivering high-velocity digital products, AI automation, and resilient cloud systems without the bloated overhead of traditional tech agencies.

Under his leadership, ZYANIX has achieved a flawless zero-complaint track record across 30+ completed projects by prioritizing radical transparency, signed MVP scope clarity, 30/30/40 milestone payments, and rigorous software engineering standards.
