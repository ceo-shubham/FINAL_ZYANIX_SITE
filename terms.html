Contractual Commitments

Terms of Service.

Last updated: August 2026. Standard terms governing client engagements, delivery guarantees, and intellectual property transfer.

1. Engagement & Signed MVP Scope

All software engineering, web development, AI automation, and consulting engagements with ZYANIX are governed by a mutually agreed Signed MVP Scope Document. Development commences upon execution of this document and receipt of the initial 30% project commencement milestone.

2. The 7-Day Delivery Guarantee

For projects designated as eligible for the 7-day guarantee within the Signed MVP Scope, ZYANIX commits to delivering the functional MVP within seven (7) consecutive calendar days from the date of final asset handoff.

If ZYANIX fails to deliver the agreed deliverables within this 7-day window under the guarantee terms, the client is entitled to receive the engineering work free of charge. The guarantee requires prompt client responsiveness for required domain/third-party access credentials.

3. 30 / 30 / 40 Milestone Payment Terms

Standard project fees are invoiced across three milestones:

  • 30% Commencement Milestone: Payable upon scope execution to initiate architecture and repository setup.
  • 30% Progress Milestone: Payable upon demonstrable delivery of core features on private staging.
  • 40% Final Milestone: Payable upon final QA completion, production cutover, and repository transfer.

4. Intellectual Property & Code Ownership

Upon receipt of the final 40% milestone payment, all intellectual property rights, source code, database architectures, graphics, and custom scripts created specifically for the project transfer completely to the client. ZYANIX retains no ongoing rights or royalties over client custom software.

5. Post-Launch Warranty

ZYANIX provides a complimentary post-launch warranty support period for all delivered projects to rectify any software bugs or inconsistencies directly attributable to the agreed MVP Scope Document.
