03 / Model Integration

LLM Integration & Private Models

Seamlessly weave frontier Large Language Models (OpenAI GPT-4o, Claude 3.5 Sonnet, Google Gemini) or private self-hosted open-source models (Ollama, LLaMA 3, Mistral) into your web and software applications.

RAG & Custom Chatbots

Privacy & Guardrails

Enterprise-grade AI security.

We ensure that your intellectual property and customer records are safeguarded from public model training and data leaks.

Zero Model Training On Your Data

We configure enterprise API tiers with explicit zero-retention policies so your business data is never used to train public foundational models.

Private & Air-Gapped Deployments

For organizations with strict regulatory constraints, we deploy open-weights models (Ollama / LLaMA) on private dedicated VPS or on-premise hardware.

Deterministic Structured Outputs

We enforce JSON schema validation and function calling standards, ensuring AI outputs integrate cleanly into your database without syntax errors.

Cost & Rate-Limit Protection

Intelligent caching, request throttling, and token budgeting algorithms prevent runaway API billing while preserving real-time speed.
