About ZYANIX

Built to make
progress practical.

ZYANIX is a modern technology studio building scalable digital products, business software, and cloud infrastructure for startups, SMEs, and enterprises.

Our Story

Experience first.
Ambition second.

ZYANIX is the direct successor to ZENSTRO IT Solutions — an earlier technology venture that provided practical experience delivering web development and software solutions across numerous industries. Building on that foundation, ZYANIX was established with a stronger focus on innovation, reliability, automation, artificial intelligence, cloud infrastructure, cybersecurity, and long-term technology partnerships.

We work across the complete technology lifecycle — from understanding a business problem and planning an MVP, to designing, developing, deploying, securing, and continuously improving the final product.

Our goal is to make high-quality technology accessible, affordable, scalable and fast — while maintaining professional development standards and transparent relationships with every client.

Vision

To build one of India's most trusted technology companies — delivering innovative, scalable, AI-powered solutions that help businesses grow faster.

Not just a vendor — a trusted technology partner. — ZYANIX

Founder & CEO

Shubham
Pathak.

Shubham Pathak leads ZYANIX with a simple conviction: technology should empower businesses rather than make them more complicated. Before founding ZYANIX, he founded ZENSTRO IT Solutions, where he gained practical experience delivering web and software solutions — experience that now shapes how ZYANIX designs, builds and runs every product.

His approach goes beyond delivering projects. He focuses on understanding the underlying business problem first, then designing technology that is scalable, secure, reliable and easy to maintain.

Under his leadership, ZYANIX has delivered 30+ projects, continues to run 10+ active projects, and maintains a zero-complaint track record built on quality, transparency, reliability and client satisfaction.
